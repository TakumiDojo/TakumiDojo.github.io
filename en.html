    Nihon Jujutsu

    Discover the fascinating world of Nihon Jujutsu, a traditional Japanese martial art that combines body safety with self-defense techniques. Our program offers you the opportunity to learn and master this ancient discipline while developing confidence, resilience, and physical control.

    Our highly qualified instructors will guide you through a comprehensive training program covering the basics to advanced techniques. You will learn to use your body efficiently, leveraging balance, strength, and endurance to defend yourself effectively.

    Safety is our top priority. You will learn how to fall and roll correctly, avoiding injuries and developing greater body awareness. Additionally, our self-defense techniques are designed to protect you and safeguard your physical integrity in real-life situations.

    Nihon Jujutsu is more than just a martial art; it's a way of life. As you progress in your learning, you'll develop discipline, self-control, and respect for yourself and others. You'll learn to channel your energy and stay calm in stressful situations.

    Our training program is suitable for all ages and fitness levels. Whether you're a beginner or have prior martial arts experience, our personalized approach will allow you to progress at your own pace.

    Learning Nihon Jujutsu gives you practical skills you can apply in your everyday life. It provides you with the confidence and security needed to face adversity and protect yourself and your loved ones.

    Don't miss the chance to dive into the fascinating world of Nihon Jujutsu. Join us and discover the perfect combination of safety, physical activity, and self-defense. Dare to take the first step and begin your journey towards mastering your body and protecting your well-being!

    Aikijujutsu

    Discover the fascinating world of Aikijujutsu, a traditional Japanese martial art that combines self-defense techniques with principles of harmony and fluidity. Our program gives you the opportunity to immerse yourself in the practice of this ancient art and acquire a solid foundation in martial arts.

    Our highly trained instructors will guide you through a comprehensive program covering basic to advanced Aikijujutsu techniques. You will learn to harness the power of energy and balance to control your opponent efficiently and effectively.

    The practice of Aikijujutsu not only provides self-defense skills but also fosters personal development. As you immerse yourself in this discipline, you'll cultivate values such as respect, discipline, perseverance, and humility. You'll learn to channel your inner energy and maintain calm in challenging situations.

    Safety is our top priority. Throughout your training, you'll learn techniques adapted to avoid injuries and develop deeper body awareness. Our approach is based on progressive teaching in a safe and respectful training environment.

    Aikijujutsu practice has no age or physical prerequisites. Our program adapts to each individual, allowing you to progress at your own pace and reach your full potential. Whether you're a beginner or have martial arts experience, Aikijujutsu offers you a continuous learning path and personal growth.

    Immerse yourself in Aikijujutsu and discover the physical, mental, and spiritual benefits this ancient discipline has to offer. Join our community of committed students and experience the beauty of harmony and power in Aikijujutsu. Don't miss this opportunity to embark on a transformative and enriching martial arts journey!

    Our History

    In 2010, we took our first steps in Sant Feliu de Guíxols, after starting our journey in Palamós. With great enthusiasm, we founded the Nihon Kobudo Sant Feliu Club, marking the beginning of our adventure in this new location.

    Over the years, the two founders grew both in their practice and vision, and a small team of passionate practitioners gradually formed. This team became the foundation for the creation of the first dojo, Takumi Dojo, located on Carrer Bourg de Peage.

    After a decade of dedication and effort, and due to life circumstances, Takumi Dojo continued to expand, both in membership and spirit. With a wonderful team twice the size of the original, we reached a new milestone: the establishment of Takumi Dojo in its current location on Carrer Sant Adolf, 23. This step represents the culmination of a challenge and a commitment to continuous improvement.

    Today, we remain focused on growth, maintaining the same spirit of self-improvement and community that has characterized us since the beginning.
